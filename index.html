Privacy Policy

Lesson Ledger for iPhone · Last updated October 25, 2026

The short version

Lesson Ledger keeps your students, lessons and payments on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud account. We don't run servers that receive this data, we don't sell or share it, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores

  • Students' names, subjects and lesson times you enter.
  • Parents' names and phone numbers you enter, used only to prefill messages you choose to send.
  • Lesson outcomes, packages, payments and your payment handles (e.g. Venmo username).

This data lives in the app's storage on your device. With iCloud sync on, Apple stores it in your private CloudKit database, which only your Apple ID can access. We, the developer, cannot read it.

Messages and pay links

Reminders are sent from your own phone number through Apple's Messages app, only when you tap Send. Pay links point to your own Venmo, PayPal, Cash App or Monzo account; payments happen entirely within those services, and Lesson Ledger never sees payment details.

Subscriptions

Lesson Ledger Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase; we receive no payment card information.

Deleting your data

Archive or delete students in the app, or delete the app to remove local data. To remove synced data, delete the app's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

The app is meant for teachers. It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect information from them.
